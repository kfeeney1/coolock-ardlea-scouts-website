# Website GDPR & Data Protection Report

**Report to Coolock Ardlea Scouts Group Council - 12 September 2026**

This report explains how the Scout Group website handles personal information, the protections currently in place, the remaining decisions for Group Council, and the actions recommended to support good data-protection governance. It is not legal advice or a certification of GDPR compliance.

## Overall position

The website has strong technical controls around personal information. Access to member, parent, consent, medical, attendance, gallery and financial information is restricted according to role and relationship to the relevant young person or section. Public information is kept separate from private member records.

A recent review did not identify a Critical or High unauthorised exposure of child or medical information. The main remaining work is governance: the Group should formally approve its privacy wording, retention periods and data-handling procedures.

## Group Council decisions required

- Approve the final privacy notice and Group lawful-basis wording.
- Approve retention periods for relevant categories of personal information.
- Approve the procedure for access, correction, restriction, deletion and other data-protection requests.
- Confirm who within the Group owns privacy and retention decisions.
- Periodically review the external processors used by the website.
